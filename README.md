## AI In-House Development & Business Workflows

I work with people who understand their business, turning operational knowledge into requirements, workflows, and working software.

My approach is built around five principles:

- **Start with real problems.** Identify recurring tasks, bottlenecks, and operational pain points before choosing AI solutions.
- **Redesign the workflow.** Rethink how work should be done instead of simply automating existing processes.
- **Build in-house capabilities.** Create systems that teams can operate and improve without relying entirely on external vendors.
- **Design for reliability.** Combine AI agents with automated validation, human approvals, monitoring, and failure handling.
- **Expand gradually.** Start with well-understood tasks, validate results, and scale from individuals to teams and departments.
